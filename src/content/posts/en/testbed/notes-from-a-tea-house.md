The tea house sits on a narrow street. There is no sign over the door, only a wooden board with the character for tea written on it.

## Two in the afternoon

Few people are inside. At the window table two old men are playing chess, and every so often a piece lands on the wooden board. The owner comes over with a copper kettle, tops up the water, and goes back behind the counter to a television turned down very low.

## Four in the afternoon

Light slants in through the window and falls on the rim of the cup. The tea has been refilled three times; the colour is paler, and the taste somehow clearer.

## On the way out

When I pay, the owner says it may rain tomorrow, and rainy days bring more people. I say I will come back on a sunny day instead.
