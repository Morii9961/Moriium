I make a reading list at the start of every year, and finishing half of it by December counts as a good year. This year's list is shorter than usual, in three groups.

## Literature

| Title | Author | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Essays in Idleness* | Yoshida Kenkō | To see how someone wrote essays seven hundred years ago |
| *Invisible Cities* | Italo Calvino | I hear every chapter is short, which suits bedtime |
| *The Remains of the Day* | Kazuo Ishiguro | I have meant to read it for years |

## Design

- *Designing Design*, Kenya Hara.
- *The Elements of Typographic Style*, Robert Bringhurst. It came up again and again while this site was being built, so it deserves a proper read.

## Computing

- *Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs*. At university I only read the first two chapters.

Each time I finish one, I will come back and strike out a line.
